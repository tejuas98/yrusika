# Brag Plan: Yrusika — AI Video Note Taker

## The Angle
Students and engineers waste hours pausing technical videos, taking messy screenshots, and wrestling with paywalled note-taking extensions that lock notes after 20 minutes. Yrusika shatters this barrier: a 100% free, open-source Chromium extension that docks directly alongside YouTube, grabs high-res slides with one click, and syncs clean Markdown directly into your local Obsidian vault.

## The Hook (0.0s – 3.2s)
"Stop pausing lectures to write notes."
Dark, cinematic opening with high-contrast kinetic typography transitioning into an active YouTube coding lecture.

## Highlights
1. **Seamless Native Split-Screen (3.2s – 7.5s):** The video player smoothly resizes and the Yrusika note workstation docks on the right with dark Obsidian aesthetics, `< Yrusika >` header, and the mascot avatar.
2. **Instant Slide & Keyframe Capture (7.5s – 12.0s):** Click the Camera icon `[ 📷 ]` to capture code slides and visual diagrams at high resolution with burned timestamp badges (`▶ 00:01:58`) and interactive seek tooltips.
3. **Local Obsidian Vault Stream (12.0s – 16.0s):** Direct file system sync (`Vault: notes`). No cloud locks, no subscriptions. Clean `.md` files stream directly into your local Obsidian graph.

## The Punchline / Outro (16.0s – 20.0s)
"100% Free Forever. Zero Paywalls. MIT Licensed."
Closing badge with glowing Yrusika mascot, GitHub repository link (`github.com/tejuas98/yrusika`), and call to action: "Load Unpacked & Start Studying."
